A Message from Sam Sugarman

October 14, 2026

To the visitor:

Thank you for stopping by. This page is maintained by one person, in the San Fernando Valley, in the hours left over from running a company. It is plain on purpose. The one modern feature is the sound, which is off until you turn it on.

By day I am the founder of Mulholland, Inc., in Burbank. We build software that takes over the parts of office work that people retype by hand, and we run it for them. The company has a website of its own with the particulars.

Before that I was a guitarist, producer and writer. I played in Childish Gambino's band and wrote, produced and played on records, which are listed under Music.

On weekends I keep a family archive, which is private, and a list of things I like about the Valley, which is not.

Sincerely,

Sam Sugarman
