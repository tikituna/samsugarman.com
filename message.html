A Message from Sam Sugarman

October 15, 2026

To the visitor:

Thank you for stopping by. This page is kept by one person, in the San Fernando Valley.

I am a husband, and the father of Jack and Stella, who are four and two. I founded Mulholland, Inc., an AI-native services company. We build software that takes over large parts of a business’s work, and we sell the result as a service. The particulars are on the company’s own site.

Before all that I made records. I was a guitarist, producer and writer, I played in Childish Gambino’s band, and I wrote, produced and played on a good many things you may have heard. Some of them are listed here.

I was born in Tarzana, and I live in Studio City now, a few miles east along the same boulevard. Most evenings there are records playing in the garage, and some of them go out over San Fernando Valley Radio, which you can hear from the home page. I also keep an archive for my family, so that the people in the old photographs stay known. If any of this gives you a reason to write, hello@samsugarman.com reaches me.

Sincerely,

Sam Sugarman
