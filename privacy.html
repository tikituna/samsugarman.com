Privacy Policy
Effective November 4, 2026

This is a small site and it collects very little. There are no accounts, no cookies, no analytics, no ads and no trackers.

What your browser keeps. If you turn the sound on, your browser remembers that for next time. It stays on your computer and is never sent to me. Clear your browser's site data and it is gone.

Who else sees your visit. The site is hosted by GitHub Pages, which, like any web host, receives your IP address and keeps its own server logs under GitHub's privacy statement. The music is served from GitHub too. The home page asks the San Fernando Valley Radio station whether it is on the air, so that server sees your IP address as well. The Credits page shows pictures from YouTube, so YouTube sees those requests.

If you write. When you email hello@samsugarman.com, your message and address stay in my inbox. I do not sell, rent or share them, and I will not add you to a mailing list.

Text messages. If you agree to receive text messages from Sugarman Ltd., your mobile number and the record of your consent are used only to send you those messages. Mobile numbers and text message consent are not sold or shared with third parties or affiliates for their marketing. Reply STOP to any message to stop receiving them. See the Terms of Use.

Changes. If this changes, this page will change and so will the date above.

Questions, or want your email deleted? Write to hello@samsugarman.com.
