Mulholland, Inc.
Updated October 14, 2026

Mulholland is the company I founded and run. Its offices are in Burbank, California. Its mission is autonomy for every business. In more words, from its own site: to transform work inside companies to accelerate their progress and prosperity.

The company manufactures and deploys operational services as software. A customer keeps running their business; Mulholland runs the AI that does the repetitive part of it, such as checking a patient's insurance overnight, turning a stack of time sheets into a pay run for a person to approve, or matching lab reports to the products they certify. Every action the software takes leaves a receipt that says what it read, what it wrote and who approved it.

The product is called Marzy. The company also trains its own open-weight models for the paperwork of particular industries; the first, Vulcan 2.5, was published in September 2026.
