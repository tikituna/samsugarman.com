San Fernando Valley, California

The San Fernando Valley
Updated October 14, 2026

The San Fernando Valley is the flat part of Los Angeles north of the hills. About 1.8 million people live in it. The ridge road that divides it from the rest of the city is Mulholland Drive. The long road that runs the length of it is Ventura Boulevard. Burbank, where Mulholland, Inc. has its offices, is in the Valley's eastern end.
